CSIR–CSIO Chandigarh

Research Intern

The work
Deep learning models for stereo image depth analysis, built and trained from scratch rather than fine-tuned, so I had to understand what each layer contributed instead of treating the network as a black box.
Approach
Explored several routes in parallel — monocular depth estimation and YOLO-based detection among them — and compared them against each other under a fixed research timeline.
What it taught me
That most of the difficulty in an ML problem is upstream of the model: how the data is captured, aligned and cleaned decides the ceiling before training starts. That lesson is why I ended up doing pipeline work.

Kudzu Infotech — Contractor for Deloitte (Goa IDC)

Full Stack Developer

The platform
Python backend services powering a RAG-based LLM compliance agent — Llama models orchestrated through LangChain — for the Goa Industrial Development Corporation.
Data problem
ETL pipelines using ezdxf over multi-layer industrial drawing files, extracting, validating and transforming geometry so that floor plans, site plans and elevations come out consistent regardless of how the source file was organised.
Models
CNN-based entity detection, StyleGAN for synthetic training data where real samples were thin, and Graph Neural Networks modelling relationships between drawing elements so the compliance reasoning sees structure rather than isolated shapes.
Integration
Stitching CV models, LLM services and structured data pipelines together through internal APIs, then troubleshooting and tuning them as the system scales.
How it's delivered
A Deloitte-contracted, compliance-audited model, working in a cross-functional team with product managers, designers and AI engineers on Agile-aligned review cycles — the constraint shapes the engineering as much as the requirements do.
